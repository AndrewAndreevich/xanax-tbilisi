ABOUT

XANAX TBILISI Alisa Yoffe, painter, Arseniy Morozov, musician, and Andrey Aranovich, game designer, met in Tbilisi after leaving Russia in the beginning of March 2022 due to their protest against the full scale invasion in Ukraine.Their anti-Putin and anti-war beliefs formed a band’s attitude.

Last six months they are living as emigrants in Tbilisi, a capital of Sakartvelo, former Georgian Soviet Socialist Republic, where the Russian armed forces are occupying nowadays a territory of the Abkhazia and South Ossetia regions.

A first band’s single has become a statement that Putin Is Dead.

In the middle of the track there is a Putin’s speech in German about the European integration, in which he tells about lessons of the Cold War and perniciousness of an occupation ideology.

”Russia is an occupant” and “Russians go home’ graffiti, homeless dogs, cryptocurrency exchange points were digitalized and used in building of the XANAX TBILISI world on the VR-chat platform.

Launching of the world will take place in the beginning of November, so does the first band’s concert, where band members will perform as avatars.

Besides that, a NFT with a cover of the “Putin Is Dead’ single is expected to drop.

VRCHAT

VrWorld XNX TBS is created using IT technologies on the VRCHAT platform and inherits Guy Debord's Theory of the Drift. Virtual city space was build using photographs of various locations of Tbilisi. Real streets, including obscene political statements on its walls, were projected onto virtually generated facades of buildings. Members of XANAX TBILISI music band exist there as digital avatars. Final release of the world will take place on the VRChat platform. Band's concerts will also take place there.
